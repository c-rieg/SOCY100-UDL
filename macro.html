Macrosociology vs. Microsociology

🌏︎

Macro: Marx, Weber, and Durkheim are macro sociologists

  • Concentrates on large-scale phenomena or entire civilizations
  • Example: Durkheim analyzed suicide rates across several major countries
🔍︎

Micro:

  • Interested in small scale interactions between human beings and groups on an everyday basis
  • Stresses study of small groups and the analysis of our everyday experiences, human agency, and interpersonal interactions

Comparing Macro and Micro Research

Example: explain a religious cult

  • Macro: Let's look at a lot of cults and see if there is a pattern and explain how they sustain and get formed.
  • Micro: Go into the cult and talk to people and see what their experience is.
Macro looks at statistics and large patterns
Micro looks at the human interactions behind the numbers.
